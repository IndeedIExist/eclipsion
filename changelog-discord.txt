**ENCORE — CHANGELOG**
*20–21 July*

__**Lathes & Faction Machines**__

**Fixed**
- Lathes and microforges ignored machine part upgrades. Better manipulators speed up printing and better matter bins cut material cost again.
- Severe server lag when printing large batches in lathes and ore processors.

**Changed**
- Research servers are now faction-locked. A lathe only draws research from its own faction's server, so dropping a microforge onto someone else's grid no longer grants access to their research.
- Machines you construct now belong to your faction rather than to whatever grid they were built on.
- Imperial and Union microforges can now only be operated by members of their own faction.

**Added**
- Lathe recipes are now sorted into categories, including new Armor, Clothing, Medical, Ship parts, Power and Implants filters. The microforge's recipe list is browsable instead of one flat alphabetical wall.


__**Stock Market**__

**Changed**
- Reworked the sector stock market. The four faction companies no longer drift at random — their price only moves when something actually happens in the round, such as casualties, a station falling, treasury swings or a won war. Reading the war correctly is now the whole skill of trading them.
- Companies whose faction is not fielded in the current game mode are delisted for the round. Existing holdings are untouched and still show under your portfolio, they just cannot be traded until that faction is back.

**Added**
- Two neutral companies, the Free Merchants Network and Trans-Cluster Construction, which profit from the chaos the factions generate and give traders somewhere to hedge.
- Share prices and player portfolios now survive round and server restarts. A faction that spent three rounds losing starts the fourth one cheap.
- A news tab in the stock market cartridge, explaining why each price moved.
- Going to war now seizes your holdings in the enemy faction's company. The forced sale pays the floor price rather than the market price, so being caught holding enemy stock when the shooting starts is a real loss.
- A stocks category in the admin economy tab, and a `stockmarket` console command for listing, freezing, resetting and shocking the market.


__**Fixes**__

- Faction relations now survive round and server restarts, and the faction status widget fills in as soon as you join instead of staying blank until someone works a console.
- Radio TTS played a separate overlapping copy of every line, one per nearby listener.
- TTS now starts at a low volume instead of being silent by default, and no longer errors when a speaker disconnects mid-sentence.
- The `adminvoice` command found no voices on packaged servers.
- Shuttle console ID cards are now locked unless the console is in captain access mode, and are ejected when the console is closed.
- Admin permissions, poker hand names and diplomacy labels broke on servers whose host machine runs a Turkish system locale.
- Translated the remaining Russian text in the alert console interface.
