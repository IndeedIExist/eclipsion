Welcome to Hullrot: Eclipsion.

This is a roleplay-heavy server, not a round-based deathmatch. You are not a lone crewmember on a company station - you are one person living in Taypan, a cold and unforgiving stretch of frontier space where rival factions, ships and crews fight, trade and survive. Everyone who joins helps build that world, so take your character and the setting seriously.

Before anything else: open the Guidebook (press Numpad 0, or click the Guidebook button in the game menu) and read the Rules section in full. It holds both the server rules and the Roleplay Rules, which cover mutiny, round removal and hate speech. You are expected to know them before you join a round.

You belong to a faction and a role within it. Play that role, follow your chain of command, and stay in character. Taypan is dangerous: you will die, and you will lose the gear you were carrying. Do not let that get to you - it is part of the setting.

If you have questions, ask your crew in person or over the radio. If you need an admin - for example if you think someone is breaking the rules - press Escape > Admin Help (ahelp) and message the staff.

Instead of clicking to attack, we use Combat Mode. Check the Controls section below for its keybind. With it off you cannot harm anyone, so you will not hit yourself with your own tools.
