[color=#ffffff]In Character and Out of Character[/color]

If you are new to roleplaying, read this section. If not, skip ahead.

At its core this is a roleplaying game. You play a character you build in the lobby before joining. While you are in a round you are [bold]IC[/bold] (In Character): your character only knows what they have personally seen and heard, and behaves as a real person in that setting would. Forget anything you know from previous rounds or from outside the game.

[bold]OOC[/bold] (Out Of Character) is anything outside the game world - the OOC chat, Discord, or talking with friends. Do not carry OOC information into the round, and do not bring round events into OOC while the round is still running. Using outside knowledge to gain an in-game advantage is [bold]metagaming[/bold], and it will get you banned. Example: a friend on Discord tells you where an enemy is hiding and you go straight there. If you think someone is metagaming, report it with ahelp.

[color=#ffffff]Conflict and Combat[/color]

There are no secret "antagonists" rolled at the start of the round here. Taypan is a frontier at war: every faction has its own goals, and conflict between them is expected and encouraged. You do not need special permission to fight an enemy faction - but it should grow out of the story, not out of boredom.

Killing a player ends a conflict quickly, but try to make it mean something first. A living hostage is almost always worth more than a corpse, and turning every round into a deathmatch gets stale for everyone.

Two rules matter most, and both are explained in full in the Roleplay Rules (Guidebook, Numpad 0):

 - Never turn on your own faction's leadership ([bold]mutiny[/bold]) without prior admin approval.

 - Never [bold]round-remove[/bold] - permanently destroying a body so it can never be revived - a member of your own faction, under any circumstance. Between enemy factions in active combat it is allowed.

Read the Guidebook's Rules section before you play. When in doubt, ask an admin with ahelp.
